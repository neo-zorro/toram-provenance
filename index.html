WORKING FINANCIAL RUNTIME

Financial intelligence needs an operating layer. TORAM keeps state, authority, action and evidence coherent.

TORAM captures causal market state, applies intelligence, authorizes actions inside deterministic limits, executes through controlled broker adapters and preserves a replayable evidence chain.

The first reference system is NIFTY futures and options. The platform is being generalized into a multi-instrument financial runtime without making any single model, feed or broker the product boundary.

CURRENT PROOF

A working reference runtime, not a strategy mockup.

TORAM discovered the platform requirements by solving one difficult derivatives market end to end. The current system spans durable capture, one causal live/replay decision path, controlled execution, reconciliation and post-trade reconstruction.

PARITY

WHY NIFTY FIRST

A proving ground where failure is immediate and measurable.

NIFTY futures/options force one system to handle non-stationary data, changing liquidity, strict market sessions, options microstructure, capital limits, live/historical parity and ambiguous execution. The goal was to discover the contracts a reusable financial runtime actually needs.

Time matters.

Stale state can turn a correct idea into a wrong action.

Authority matters.

Confidence must never silently become permission.

Evidence matters.

A system that cannot reconstruct why it acted cannot safely scale authority.
