A broker-connected execution and risk workspace for supported exchange-traded instruments. It turns market and position state into governed actions without making one proprietary trading model the customer-facing product.

WHY NIFTY OPTIONS FIRST

A proving ground where failure is immediate and measurable.

NIFTY futures/options force one system to handle non-stationary data, changing liquidity, strict market sessions, options microstructure, capital limits, live/historical parity and ambiguous execution. The point was not to stay NIFTY-only; it was to discover the contracts a reusable financial runtime actually needs.

Time matters.

Stale state can turn a correct idea into a wrong action.

Authority matters.

Confidence must never silently become permission.

Evidence matters.

A system that cannot reconstruct why it acted cannot safely scale authority.
