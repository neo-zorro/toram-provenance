We built the hard first system. Now we are turning it into infrastructure.

First reference system: NIFTY futures and options. The long-term objective is a repeatable financial runtime for controlled users, brokers and institutions — with broader runtime portability only after finance is proven at product scale.

WHY NIFTY OPTIONS FIRST

We chose a domain where failure is immediate and measurable.

NIFTY futures/options force one system to handle non-stationary data, changing liquidity, multi-leg execution, capital limits, strict market sessions, live/historical parity and ambiguous fills. That makes finance a demanding proving ground for the runtime.

Time matters.

Stale state can turn a correct idea into a wrong action.

Authority matters.

Confidence must never silently become permission.

Evidence matters.

A system that cannot reconstruct why it acted cannot safely scale authority.
