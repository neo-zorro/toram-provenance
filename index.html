FINANCE FIRST

Financial markets are the proving ground — not the company boundary.

Markets combine non-stationary time series, continuous feedback, strict permissions, capital risk, ambiguous execution and measurable outcomes. That makes finance a demanding place to prove the runtime primitives before generalizing them.

TORAM's first reference implementation is built around NIFTY futures/options. The public thesis does not assume that one strategy, market or broker defines the company.
