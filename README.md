# Public concept page

**An answer should earn its authority.**

This repository contains a deliberately simple public page about a future product direction:

> Ask a question. Build the temporary system needed to test the claim. If the evidence holds, keep it. If it does not, discard it. If the answer can change, keep the cycle alive.

The concept is not a conventional search engine and not a universal truth machine.

It explores a different interaction:

1. ask a question;
2. define the claim precisely;
3. gather the evidence that can legitimately support it;
4. verify source, role, method, date and context;
5. challenge the claim with conflicting evidence, failure and missing proof;
6. return a visible status such as supported, partly supported, conflicted, unproven or stale; and
7. delete, pause, keep, clone or maintain the resulting system.

## Reach is not authority

A page can rank first. A review can receive thousands of likes. A post can spread across many networks. A product claim can come from a famous company. An image, audio clip or video can look completely real.

None of those facts alone proves the claim.

The concept therefore separates **reach signals** from **authority signals**.

Reach signals include things such as:

- ranking position;
- likes, shares, views or followers;
- repeated copies;
- paid promotion;
- popularity; and
- confident presentation.

Authority signals must be tied to the claim itself, such as:

- an original source or record;
- a named creator or content holder;
- first-hand observation or use;
- a verifiable purchase, deployment or operating role;
- an attached document, measurement or result;
- independent reproduction or corroboration;
- time, version and conditions;
- visible conflicts and failures; and
- correction, withdrawal, expiry or revocation history.

## The authority layer

The long-term moat is not ownership of truth.

It is the reusable **authority infrastructure** that can preserve and evaluate the evidence around a claim.

That layer can keep:

- the claim and its exact wording;
- who originated it;
- who observed, built, used, verified, operated or commercialized it;
- what evidence is attached;
- which evidence is first-party, first-hand, independent or derived;
- who disputes it and why;
- whether the content was edited or generated;
- when the claim was valid;
- whether it has become stale, superseded, withdrawn or revoked; and
- the full history of how its authority changed.

A review, post, message, product statement, image, audio clip, video or AI-generated narrative without this chain is not automatically false. It is simply **unproven as authority**.

## Example: reviews

A star rating is not proof of use.

An authority-aware review could show:

- who is reviewing;
- whether they actually purchased, used, operated or supported the product;
- when and under what conditions;
- what evidence supports the experience;
- whether they have a commercial interest; and
- whether the experience can be corroborated.

Anonymous or unsupported reviews can remain discoverable without being promoted into authoritative evidence.

## Example: product claims

A product maker may be a primary authority for its own published specification at a stated time and version.

That does not automatically make it authoritative for every independent claim about reliability, performance, customer experience or comparative superiority.

Those require different evidence and often different witnesses.

## Example: posts and synthetic media

A platform can prove that content was posted or transmitted through its service. That does not by itself prove the statement inside the content.

Likewise, AI can generate convincing text, audio, images and video. Realistic presentation should not inherit authority automatically.

Where available, creator identity, content ownership, capture history, edit/generation history, source material and supporting evidence should travel with the claim.

## The core principle

**Build the system that makes a claim earn its authority.**

Authority attaches to a specific claim under specific conditions. It does not automatically attach to a whole website, company, person, platform or model.

## The role of AI

AI can do the heavy lifting around the cycle:

- search and gather;
- trace sources;
- compare methods and dates;
- identify missing evidence;
- surface credible disagreement;
- organize first-hand experience;
- detect copied or derivative claims; and
- keep the cycle current.

AI should not create authority merely by sounding certain.

## Temporary by design

The proof system itself can be temporary.

The user can:

- delete it when the question is over;
- pause it while keeping the work;
- retain it for a recurring need;
- clone the authority pattern for another question; or
- have it maintained when the answer can change over time.

## Public disclosure boundary

This page intentionally avoids publishing:

- private implementation details;
- internal architecture;
- proprietary methods or models;
- investor materials or financing details; and
- unsupported claims that every question can be resolved or made authoritative.

This is a public concept and product-direction page. The concept is still being proven.
